The Problem & Our Approach

⚠️ The Real-World Dilemma

  • Static City Averages Fail: Standard weather forecasts quote single city numbers (e.g. "Mumbai 31°C, AQI 115") that mask hyper-local differences.
  • Place and Time are Inseparable: A ground downwind of an expressway at 8:00 AM rush hour may be serene by 5:30 PM once the breeze shifts.
  • Personal Vulnerability: Weather impacts vary drastically for someone with mild asthma, a senior citizen, or young children.

💡 Our Neuro-Symbolic Approach

  • Deterministic Geo-Math Engine: Live OpenStreetMap Overpass extraction, Open-Meteo hourly weather/AQI, and OSRM rush-hour transit routing.
  • Open-Weights ≤3B SLM (Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct): Runs locally to parse intent, enforce guardrails, and synthesize grounded recommendations without hallucination.
  • Zero Pre-computed Data: 100% fetched live at query time from open and disclosed APIs.
Core Architecture: User Query ➔ Free-form Intent Parser ➔ Parallel Live Telemetry (OSM + Meteo + OSRM) ➔ Angular Plume & Traffic Math ➔ ≤3B SLM Grounded Synthesis

End-to-End Real-Time Architecture

Stage 1: Intent & Completeness Guardrails

  • Extracts 10+ reference outdoor activities (running, cycling, golf, cricket, tennis, hiking, photography, etc.).
  • Detects personal profiles: asthma, senior, child, pregnancy.
  • Strict guardrails: Gracefully handles missing locations or vague times without guessing.

Stage 2: Live Parallel Telemetry

  • Asynchronous parallel httpx requests with 30-minute diskcache TTL.
  • Overpass API queries parks, tracks, pitches, arterial roads, and water bodies.

Stage 3: Spatio-Temporal Plume Engine

  • Computes compass bearings between candidate venues and arterial highways/industrial zones.
  • Evaluates wind direction vectors relative to pollution sources.
  • Applies rush-hour traffic travel multipliers and canopy cooling credits.

Stage 4: Grounded SLM Synthesis

  • Qwen2.5-3B synthesizes advice strictly conditioned on live JSON telemetry numbers.
  • Interactive Leaflet map displays pins, wind compass vectors, and transit routes.

Our ≤3B Model & Zero-Hallucination Design

Model Architecture & Hosting

  • Base Model: Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct (3.09B active parameters).
  • License: Apache 2.0 (Open-source weights).
  • Hosted on Hugging Face Hub: Model card, configuration, and inference pipeline published for transparency.
  • Strict Policy: No GPT-4, Claude, or Gemini Pro used in the running assistant.

Elimination of Hallucinations

  • No Parametric Weather Guessing: Small models cannot memorise shifting weather; this limitation forces 100% reliance on live fetched data.
  • Structured Evidence Injection: The SLM receives exact calculated metrics (AQI, temperature, distance, bearing, downwind flag) via JSON prompt injection.
  • Refusal of Incomplete Requests: Asking "Is it okay to cycle to work at 8 AM?" prompts a clarifying question rather than an invented route.

Honest Limitations & Future Roadmap

Honest Limitations

  • OSM Tag Density: Informal neighborhood grounds in smaller Indian cities may lack sport=* or leisure=pitch tags in OpenStreetMap.
  • Angular Plume Approximation: Uses angular sector matching (±45°) and distance decay rather than 3D street-canyon fluid dynamics (CFD).
  • Heuristic Rush-Hour Multipliers: Traffic transit scaling is estimated over OSRM free-flow rather than live fleet GPS probes.

Future Road Map

  • Indian Open Data Ingestion: Connect directly to CPCB ground monitoring stations via data.gov.in for ground-truth AQI calibration.
  • Bilingual LoRA Fine-Tuning: Fine-tune Qwen2.5-3B on Indian outdoor sports idioms in English and Hindi.
  • Micro-Topography (SRTM): Ingest elevation models for trail gradient analysis on hikes and cycle rides.
