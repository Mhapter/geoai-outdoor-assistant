Slide 2 / 6 • The Problem & Challenge

Why Naive Weather Apps & Generic LLMs Fail

Outdoor decisions require evaluating multiple places across multiple upcoming hours rather than a single generic city weather reading.

❌ Traditional Solutions

• Weather apps give a single city-wide temperature, ignoring local park micro-climates.

• Hosted LLMs (GPT-4) hallucinate park names, stale temperatures, and fake routes.

• Blind to wind direction relative to expressways and toxic industrial exhaust plumes.

• Inability to adapt to personal vulnerabilities such as asthma or elderly mobility needs.

✅ The Fieldwise Approach

• Evaluates top candidate venues against 24-48 hourly atmospheric vectors simultaneously.

• Physics-based downwind plume detection using trigonometric wind bearing math.

• Real OSRM driving durations accounting for peak rush-hour transit penalties.

• SLM synthesizes actionable advice (what to carry, what to monitor, when to avoid).

Slide 4 / 6 • Worked Example Walkthrough

Real-World Verification: Asthma Jogging Query

"I have mild asthma. Where would be suitable for a walk this evening near Powai, and what time should I go?"

Execution Pipeline

1. Intent Parser: Activity = Walking/Jogging, Location = Powai (Mumbai), Window = Evening (17:00-20:00), Profile = Asthma.

2. Spatial Discovery: Fetches Powai Lake Promenade, Hiranandani Gardens, Sambhaji Park, and Vihar Lake Forest.

3. Wind & Plume Modeling: Prevailing wind at 18:00 is 14 km/h from NW. JVLR Expressway is north of venue. Calculates angular diff: venue is clear of vehicle exhaust.

Synthesized Recommendation

Top Pick: Powai Lake Promenade at 06:00 PM (Score: 88/100).

Supporting Evidence: US AQI 58 (PM2.5: 16.2 µg/m³), 26.4°C, evaporative cooling from lake, shaded pedestrian canopy.

Carry & Watch: Fast-acting rescue inhaler (albuterol), N95 mask for transit. Avoid 08:00 PM when evening rush-hour plume arrives.

Slide 6 / 6 • Limitations & Ethics

Transparent Limitations & Honest Scope

Ethical boundaries and operational considerations for Fieldwise.

Technical & Data Limitations

• Free-Tier Rate Limits: Overpass API and Open-Meteo require bounded caching (30m TTL) to prevent throttling.

• OSRM Free Flow: Routing durations do not model live dynamic traffic congestion unless a paid telematics feed is attached.

• OSM Completeness: OpenStreetMap data in rural areas may lack detailed footpath tags.

Safety & Health Ethics

• Non-Clinical Scope: Personal profile limits (asthma, seniors) are conservative guidelines and do not constitute medical diagnosis.

• Privacy First: Personal constraints are ephemeral and never logged or stored in a persistent database.

• Honest Uncertainty: The system explains when insufficient candidate venues prevent reliable ranking.
