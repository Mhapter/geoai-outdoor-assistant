The Geospatial Dilemma: Why City Weather & Large LLMs Fail

An outdoor decision depends simultaneously on time, micro-climate, and surrounding vector geometry.

❌ Why Traditional Apps & LLMs Fail
  • Single City Centroid: Weather apps claim "Mumbai is 29°C, Moderate Air". They are completely blind to a park sitting 40m downwind of an arterial highway exhaust plume.
  • Hallucinated Topography: GPT-4 and unconstrained LLMs fabricate non-existent stadiums, guess outdated temperatures, and confuse straight-line flight distance with road transit.
  • Stale Memorized Knowledge: A model fine-tuned on yesterday's geography cannot know that today's 14 km/h SW wind will blow industrial factory emissions directly onto a sports ground.
✅ The Fieldwise Neuro-Symbolic Solution
  • Multi-Hour, Multi-Venue Scans: Discovers candidate venues across a requested radius and scores every upcoming hour simultaneously.
  • Vector Trigonometry for Downwind Dispersion: Calculates angular difference between prevailing wind direction and surrounding industrial or expressway vectors.
  • Real Road Transit vs Flight Line: Integrates OSRM routing engine to compute realistic driving times rather than naive geometric distances.

Case Study: Asthma Jogging Query in Powai

"I have mild asthma, where's good for a morning walk near Powai in the next two days?"

Multi-Venue Comparative Evaluation
1. Powai Lake Promenade (06:00 AM) Score: 88/100 (Optimal)

US AQI 54 (PM2.5: 14.1 µg/m³), 25.2°C, 8 km/h NW wind. Upwind of JVLR highway (airflow moves away from pedestrian path). Water body evaporative cooling.

2. Hiranandani Heritage Garden (07:00 AM) Score: 71/100 (Good)

US AQI 78, 27.0°C. Sheltered by building canyon, but crosswind starts accumulating local traffic exhaust.

3. JVLR Arterial Strip (08:00 AM) Score: 24/100 (Avoid)

US AQI 168 (PM2.5: 88.4 µg/m³). Directly downwind of morning rush-hour traffic plume. Violates asthma threshold (max AQI ≤ 75).

Synthesized Practical Advisory

Top Choice: Powai Lake Promenade at 06:00 AM — superior micro-climate with upwind air corridor and natural canopy buffering.

🎒 What to Prepare & Carry:

Fast-acting rescue inhaler (albuterol/salbutamol), lightweight breathable layer for 25°C morning chill, sunglasses for early low-angle glare.

⚠️ What to Watch For:

Relative humidity exceeds 78%; monitor breathing rate if ground-level morning mist lingers near shoreline.

🛑 When to Avoid:

Avoid after 08:00 AM when peak arterial traffic spikes particulate matter across all Powai perimeter zones.

Why Base SLM + Live Grounded JSON Outperforms Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning an SLM permanently embeds static, outdated weather and coordinates into frozen weight tensors. Slide 6 / 6 • Scope & Ethics

Transparent Limitations, Ethics & Operational Scope

Honest engineering constraints disclosed in strict compliance with hackathon rules.

Technical & Data Limitations
  • Free-Tier Rate Quotas: OpenStreetMap Overpass and Open-Meteo require bounded LRU caching (30-minute TTL) to prevent API rate-limiting during high judge evaluation traffic.
  • OSRM Free-Flow Modeling: Routing durations calculate topological road network transit times but do not incorporate real-time dynamic accident/congestion feeds without commercial telematics.
  • OpenStreetMap Coverage Density: While urban centers (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru) feature dense trail polygons, tier-3 rural areas may have sparse footpath tags.
Safety & Ethical Governance
  • Non-Clinical Scope: Personal profile thresholds (Asthma, Seniors, Pregnancy) are preventive micro-climate safeguards, not medical diagnosis or clinical advice.
  • Zero Storage of PII: User health constraints and GPS coordinates are processed ephemerally in-memory and never written to permanent disk storage.
  • Full Open-Data Attribution: Complies strictly with ODbL for OpenStreetMap and CC-BY 4.0 for Open-Meteo and Wikimedia Commons imagery.
